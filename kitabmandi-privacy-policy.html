KitabMandi Privacy Policy

Buy & Sell Used Books, Notes & Study Materials Easily

Suitable for Users Age 13+

Introduction

Welcome to KitabMandi. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how KitabMandi collects, uses, protects, and manages your information when you use our mobile application.

KitabMandi is a student-focused marketplace where users can buy and sell used books, notes, magazines, and educational study materials nearby.

Information We Collect

  • Name and profile information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Location information
  • Book listing details and uploaded images
  • Chat messages between users
  • Device and app usage information

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect buyers and sellers
  • To display nearby marketplace listings
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To provide customer support
  • To maintain platform safety and security

Location Permission

KitabMandi uses location permission to show nearby books, notes, and study materials available around your location.

Camera & Storage Access

Camera and storage permissions may be used for uploading book photos, profile images, and marketplace content.

Chat & User Interaction

Users can communicate privately through in-app chat functionality. KitabMandi does not allow harmful, abusive, adult, or illegal content.

Data Security

We use secure technologies and encrypted network connections to protect user data and communications.

Third-Party Services

KitabMandi may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Google Maps Services
  • Cloud Storage Services

Age Requirement

KitabMandi is intended for users aged 13 years and above. Users under 13 should not use this application.

User Content

Users are responsible for the content they upload including:

  • Book listings
  • Images
  • Descriptions
  • Messages

Any harmful, offensive, illegal, adult, or misleading content may be removed from the platform.

Account & Data Deletion

Users may contact support if they wish to request account deletion or removal of uploaded marketplace content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
