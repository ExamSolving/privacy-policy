Privacy Policy

EduPrep Academy ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, Email Address (via Email/Password or Google Sign-In)
  • Authentication Data: Secure login via Firebase Authentication
  • Usage Data: Test scores, quiz attempts, performance analytics
  • App Activity: Coins earned, rankings, and user progress
  • Device Information: Basic device details for app performance

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide mock tests, quizzes, and learning content
  • To track your performance, accuracy, and rank
  • To manage coins and premium content access

3. Notes & Offline Access

Our app allows users to download notes for offline use. These files are stored locally on your device and are not shared with third parties.

4. Premium Content

Some features and content in the app may require coins or premium access. These are managed securely within the app and linked to your account.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services:

These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We use secure technologies and Firebase infrastructure to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure.

7. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request data deletion
  • Stop using the app anytime

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for students and learners. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.
