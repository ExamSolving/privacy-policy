Request Account Deletion

At KitabMandi, we respect your privacy and give users the option to request account deletion and removal of their data.

Users can request account deletion by contacting our support team.

How to Request Account Deletion

1️⃣ Open the KitabMandi application.
2️⃣ Go to Help & Support section.
3️⃣ Contact support with your registered email or phone number.
4️⃣ Request account deletion.
5️⃣ Our team will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

What Data May Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Uploaded book listings
  • Marketplace content
  • Chat-related data
  • Stored account information

Data Retention

Certain information may be temporarily retained where required for legal obligations, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or security purposes.
